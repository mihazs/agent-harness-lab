Add `agent_harness_lab.loop.adapters.FrozenBankEvaluator`, the loop controller's evaluator port over the existing trial runner, so the evaluation can run before an improvement and again on identical frozen inputs. It freezes the development and regression tasks of a bank (held-out and certification tasks are never run, hashed or read) and hashes the bank, grader, seed schedule, per-arm budget share and runtime into `frozen_inputs_sha256`; it observes them again around every run, and a difference ends the loop `comparison_invalid` with no verdict and an investigation artifact that names the pipeline defect. The baseline is scored from one arm and every later evaluation is a fresh paired run, so the baseline declares a two-arm reserve. A score is the mean of per-root outcomes with a `statistics.bound` interval; an outcome that is not passed or failed leaves its root unscored and keeps the evaluation provisional, and fewer roots than the plan minimum can never be accepted. The five vacuity labels become the validity gate: a vacuous baseline stops `evidence_insufficient` before any improvement and a vacuous candidate is `inconclusive`. A regression is investigated through `before_after` and blamed on the candidate or the pipeline. `Evaluation` gains `reserve_trials` and `reserve_seconds`, the ledger's evaluation summaries record the `inputs` digest, and `run_evaluation` accepts an optional `arms` argument that runs one arm; `ahl improve`, its receipt keys and its exit codes are unchanged. Part of #813.
