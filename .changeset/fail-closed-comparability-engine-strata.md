Add `agent_harness_lab.memory.comparability`, a pure offline engine that decides whether two evaluations may be compared and names every gate that stops them (part of #637). From caller-supplied facts it checks the frozen endpoint and error control, task, root and family definitions, partition, grader, bank and fixture selection, baseline, design, runtime, model settings, metric, resource and missingness gates, plus live oracle, bank, evidence, held-out and fresh-execution authority and the declared root count, and returns `comparable`, `not_comparable` (a known stratum difference) or `insufficient_evidence` (an unknown, invalid or short gate) with `ahl-comparability-v1` named failures. Unknown values (missing, empty, non-finite, unserializable, nested deeper than 8 levels or over 1 MiB of canonical JSON) never match, repeated trials of one root count once, a changed grader, bank, model or uncontrolled environment never makes a regression claim eligible, an A/A check never makes history comparable, and evaluations with different definitions are placed in separate strata. Deriving the facts from sealed histories, harness snapshots and metric contracts, and a command that runs the engine, remain open in #637.
