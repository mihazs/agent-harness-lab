Let the private diagnostic corpus record OpenSSH key approvals of submissions and adjudications (`CorpusStore.install_review_policy`, `prepare_review`, `submit_authenticated`), reusing the host reviewer-policy mechanism of the artifact and bank reviews under a new `diagnostic_corpus_review` purpose and signature namespace. An approval is bound to the exact case, current event, reviewer, label and reason, so a changed label, another key, namespace or store, or a stale event is rejected and records nothing; claims stay operator-declared and the queue works unchanged without the optional verifier. `CorpusStore.gold_label_policy()` re-verifies every review event of each current case under the frozen `diagnostic-gold-label-policy-v1` and reports `meets_policy` with blocker codes (unauthenticated review, invalid or revoked signature, reused key, unknown outcome, ineligible capture). A case that cannot be verified is `null`, never a pass or a fail. The report issues no gold label: a verified key does not show that reviewers are independent humans or that the declared outcome is true, and export, benchmark, protected-access and classifier-selection outputs are unchanged.
