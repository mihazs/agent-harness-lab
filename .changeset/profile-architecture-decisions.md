The harness profile now reads the decision records a checkout keeps (adr-tools and MADR layouts, accepted and superseded statuses only) and the cheap architectural patterns of its tree (internal dependencies between workspace packages, generated-code and migration folders). A harness line that runs a tool an accepted decision ruled out, or that still states a superseded decision, becomes a contradiction; decision records or a pattern that no instruction file mentions become gaps. They carry stable ids and counted evidence, and reach the brief, the proposer and `ahl audit` like the other profile findings. Unclear records never produce a finding.
