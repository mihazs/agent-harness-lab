Label evaluations that prove nothing and make sure none of them reports an improvement. The five labels `no_eligible_cases`, `all_outcomes_unknown`, `arms_identical`, `grader_not_executed` and `zero_trials` are one closed set in `agent_harness_lab.evaluation.vacuity`, derived from recorded trial rows and arm digests only (an unknown digest or lineage never applies a label). `ahl improve` now ends a vacuous evaluation as `inconclusive` / `evaluation_vacuous` with a `vacuity` block, before any refinement attempt, recommendation or change plan, and the `decision` block (`vacuous_evaluation`, `invalid_evidence`) and the `evidence_strength` label (`insufficient`) say so; an `evaluation_uninformative` or `missing_or_duplicate_trials` receipt carries the same block. Three bugs are fixed: a manifest with no declared task counted zero trials as complete coverage (`legacy_coverage`), an `ExperimentSpec` whose baseline and candidate hashes were equal produced a `supported` fixed analysis from noise (it is now rejected as `arms_identical`), and an experiment with equal arm hashes marked a side-by-side pair `improved` (it is now `not_comparable` with `arms_identical`). The new vacuity label reference (`docs/vacuity-labels.md`) lists each label, where every surface applies it and the audit of reported numbers; a mutation test per label breaks its producing rule and requires the matching case to fail. Part of #805.
