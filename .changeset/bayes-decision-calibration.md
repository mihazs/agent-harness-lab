Calibrate the paired Bayesian decision score with the seeded power simulator. The new `evaluation/bayes_calibration.py` freezes a protocol before any run (declared synthetic outcome models, tuning and held-out seeds, 300 simulations per cell, and the rule that the Wilson 95% upper bound of the `better` rate under a null model and under a true effect of half of `rope_epsilon` is at most .05), draws the records with the power simulator's own generator, judges each set with the unchanged `statistics.analyze` and the `bayesian_decision` block, and reports power under a beneficial model and the rates under dependent-root models without a pass or fail. The default `p_cut` of `bayes_decision` is raised once from .95 to .99, and its default `cross_check_alpha` follows to .01, because one required cell failed on the tuning seeds at .95; the held-out seeds confirm .99, and the root floor of 8 is unchanged. `task calibrate:bayes-decision SPLIT=tuning|held_out` reruns one split. The recorded synthetic results and a fast guard that fails when a change pushes the null false-recommendation rate over the bound ship as tests. The Hoeffding certification path, `promotion.policy.decide` and `automatic_promotion` are unchanged.
