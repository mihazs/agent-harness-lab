The offline `local_file/test-results/v1` source now also reads TAP 13 and 14 streams (`"format": "tap"`) bound to a declared commit. Each top-level test point becomes one case named by its description (`#N` by position when it has none): `ok` is `passed`, `not ok` is `failed`, `ok # SKIP` is `skipped`, and every result that cannot be read as a verdict stays `unknown` with its reason (`# TODO` in either direction, `not ok # SKIP`, a directive that continues the word such as `# skipped`). A plan that disagrees with the test points, a missing plan, a skipped plan, an out-of-sequence test number, indented subtest lines, unrecognized lines and a `Bail out!` (which ends the read) are warnings, never filled-in tests or failures; a stream with no plan, test point or bail out, a second plan, a plan not starting at 1 or a version other than 13 or 14 is refused. The reader is line oriented with no tree: NUL bytes, invalid UTF-8, a line over the new `max_line_length` limit (default and ceiling 4,096 bytes), a plan above `max_cases` and the first test point past `max_cases` are refused as `HostileReport`, and diagnostics, YAML and messages are never stored. Results carry the same declared-commit evidence labels as JUnit and pytest, are never task grades and still create no independent trials. Part of #53; coverage and lint reports, artifact download and zip handling, and expired-artifact warnings from a live read remain open.
