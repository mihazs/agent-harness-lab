Read recorded compactions as evidence for the `context_loss` repair candidate (part of #656). A sessions file may carry `compactions`, the turn positions where the runtime compacted the context; `ahl instruction-adherence --native` now records them from Claude Code compaction boundary and summary rows and from Codex `compacted` rows and `context_compacted` events (each compaction counted once, the rows checked like any other row of the capture). Every finding gains `compaction`: `in_window` when a recorded compaction fell after the instruction came into view and by the last turn of the finding, `none_in_window`, or `not_recorded` when the session records none. `ahl instruction-repair plan` opens `context_loss` for an early violation with a compaction in the window and names the fact in `support` (`compaction_in_window`, `no_compaction_in_window`, `compaction_not_recorded`); the basis stays `not_excluded`, because whether the compaction removed the instruction is not read, and a report from before the field reads as not recorded. A finding's identity, status and counts do not change.
