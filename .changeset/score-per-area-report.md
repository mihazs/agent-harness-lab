Add `ahl score [PROJECT] --spec FILE --records FILE [--area ID ...] [--format json|md]`, a read-only per-area report over a bank and the recorded runs of one paired experiment (schema `ahl-score-v1`). Each area declared in `ahl.json` gets one row with its tasks, distinct roots, the success rate of each recorded arm with its interval, and, when a candidate was recorded, the paired delta, its fixed-analysis status and the critical regressions. The numbers reuse `statistics.analyze` (run on the area's tasks) and the loop's `absolute_score`; no statistic is added. Every row carries one evidence state: `scored`, `provisional` (an unknown outcome or fewer roots than the plan minimum; interval, no verdict), `no_evidence` (no experiment task or no graded outcome; no number, never a zero) or `blocked` (the bank or a grader is not qualified, or the plan cannot be scored per area; no number). A missing, ungraded or repeated trial is unknown and never a pass, a fail or an independence label. An overall figure is printed only when every declared area is scored and no `--area` narrows the report. An undeclared or repeated `--area` is a typed error (`score_area_undeclared`, `area_duplicate`) that names the position and never the value. `task_bank.inspect` now accepts the project area registry, as `validate` does. Documented in `docs/score-by-capability-area.md` and `docs/score-reference.md`.
