Add an opt-in whole-cluster audit design, `diagnostics.audit_cluster`, for units that share a lineage and so cannot be declared independent. An `audit-cluster-map-v1` bound to the frame by hash must give every captured eligible unit exactly one cluster; a unit in two clusters, a unit repeated in one, an excluded, missing or foreign unit, a repeated cluster identifier, an empty cluster and an unassigned unit (unknown lineage is never a singleton) are refused. `draw` takes a declared number of whole clusters by hash rank without replacement under a hard unit budget checked against the largest clusters, so no seed can exceed it and no cluster is truncated or redrawn. `estimate` needs every unit of every drawn cluster reviewed, gives cluster-total estimates of prevalence, missed-incident rate and triage recall, and bounds them with a cluster-level Hoeffding interval, wider than an independent-unit interval by the square root of the cluster size. A missing or nonresponding review makes the result inconclusive. Receipts and reports carry hashes only, forbid publication and update no policy. The cluster design is not yet linked to corpus review, monitoring or the audit summary. Part of #75.
