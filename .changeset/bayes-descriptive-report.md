Add a descriptive desirability score and a Pareto epsilon-indicator report beside the `bayesian_decision` block (issue g of the paired Bayesian decision score). The new `evaluation/bayes_descriptive.py` puts the paired quality difference and each measured resource difference on one gain scale, prints the additive epsilon-indicator in both directions at the frozen `rope_epsilon`, the resulting relation (`candidate_dominates`, `baseline_dominates`, `epsilon_equivalent`, `trade_off` or `undetermined`) and a weighted geometric-mean desirability summary, and `ahl experiment`, the history-store analysis and `ahl compare` carry or show it as `descriptive_report` for a plan that declares `bayes_decision`. It is labelled `descriptive_only`, has no verdict or pass key and is never an input to the gate: the block is finished before it is computed, nothing in the decision path imports it, and tests show that changing a weight, the indicator or the whole report leaves the block, the fixed analysis and the promotion identical. Unmeasured usage, an undefined horizon or a decision for another contract is an unknown objective that spans the whole scale and is never read as zero. The Hoeffding certification path, `promotion.policy.decide` and `automatic_promotion` are unchanged; a plan without `bayes_decision` prints byte-identical output.
