Report interaction-contract outcomes when a saved comparison is analyzed (part of #123). A grader bundle that holds `interaction-contract.json` now adds an `interaction` section to the analysis: per family and arm, three-valued pass, interaction, whole-task and state-preservation counts, separate counts for unnecessary action, missed or unnecessary clarification, unauthorized stop (an observed stop event the contract does not authorize), missed required stop (a complete transcript with no stop event), abnormal termination, correct deliberate stop and valid non-action success, and unknowns by reason. The verdict is recomputed from the grader-declared evidence and the frozen contract; a stated outcome that contradicts it, or missing, partial or malformed evidence, leaves the observation unknown and blocks the recommendation as `interaction_observation_incomplete`, including a failure the grader states over missing evidence, which can no longer yield a statistical `rejected` (only a critical behavior-policy rejection still wins). Histories without a contract and every existing analysis result are unchanged. The evidence is a grader declaration, not an authenticated runtime capture; reply delivery and the comparison walkthrough remain open.
