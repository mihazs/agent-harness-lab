Read one report from inside a caller-supplied CI artifact ZIP (part of #53). A `test-results` or `quality-reports` declaration may add `"member"` to name one entry of the archive at its `path`; the JUnit, pytest, TAP or SARIF report is decoded by the existing parser and bounds. The archive is parsed in memory and never extracted, fetched or run, and a member is never opened as a nested archive. An archive with an unsafe entry name anywhere (absolute, `..`, backslash, drive colon, empty segment, control character), an encrypted, linked, special or ZIP64 entry, an unsupported compression method, a duplicate name, or a member whose size or checksum disagrees with its headers or exceeds the byte bound is refused whole as `HostileReport`; a declared member the archive lacks is an error, never an empty report. A report is identified by its path and member and bound by the member's own bytes, a selection that uses a member adds `archive_origin_unverified`, and `join_ci_evidence` and `join_quality_evidence` origins name the member too. Plain-file selections are unchanged. Artifact download, coverage and lint reports, and live expired-artifact warnings remain open in #53.
