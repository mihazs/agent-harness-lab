Read revert requests, reopened tasks and steering messages as weak outcome signals in `ahl instruction-adherence` (part of #655). A later user turn that names an operand a user-prompt instruction constrained and asks to revert it, says the task is not done, or comes while the agent was still acting (the turn before it is a tool call) is listed under `corroboration` of an observed violation when it follows the first violating call. Where the capture cannot decide the rule, the reactions make one `possible_violation` per instruction, named by `revert_requested`, `task_reopened`, `steering_message` or `instruction_restated`, which the calibrated build holds back as an abstention. A reaction that names nothing the rule constrains, a reply straight after the agent's question, a reaction after a supersession and a reaction to a harness rule are not read. The synthetic calibration sample gains reactions the detector cannot tell apart.
