Make the observe-only promotion monitor report honest window states and accept a sealed drift receipt without gaining any rollback authority (part of #15). `promotion.monitor.monitor` now returns `observing` while the window is open, `insufficient_evidence` once it has closed with fewer verified outcomes than the minimum exposure, and `no_alert` once it has closed with the minimum exposure and no verified critical event. Earlier releases called the last condition `stable` and called a closed window below the minimum `observing`; every result now also says `stability_claim: false`, because a closed window is not evidence that the change is stable. The monitor result is not stored in the promotion journal, so no journal record changes meaning. The new `promotion.monitor.signal(deployment, plan, reference, receipt)` checks that a `drift-receipt-v1` is sealed over its frozen plan and reference (the check is now the public `diagnostics.drift.check_receipt`, shared with `describe_task_mix`) and returns a `promotion-monitor-signal-v1` with the detector identity, window, channel states, capture coverage, uncertainty and multiplicity state. A capture, task-mix or performance alert only adds a request to investigate, review or reproduce; the signal states `deployment_attribution: not_established`, `causal_attribution: unestablished`, `automatic_rollback: false`, `rollback_authorization: false` and `promotion_authority: none`, never creates a suspected-regression or rollback state, and refuses a receipt with an unknown state, a window outside the schedule or a changed digest. Open in #15: monitor-to-`watch` integration, a durable detector-history ledger across plan resets, authenticated outcome lineage, the suspected-regression, rollback-eligible and rolled-back states, and any explicitly configured automatic rollback policy.
