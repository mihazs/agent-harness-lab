Add `ahl adapters stage` and `ahl adapters rollback`, the first command that stages a reviewed presentation adapter version and restores the previous one. The only authority is a controller-owned `ahl.adapter-authorization.v1` manifest that must live outside the project, be bound to it and not be writable by group or others. A candidate spec and its bytes are reviewed against it without executing anything: stage writes nothing and prints the candidate spec digest, a candidate that adds a tool, network destination, path, credential, nested call or a larger limit is blocked, and only the exact digest deploys the two declared files through a private journal apart from the promotion journal. Rollback previews by default and restores only with `--execute`; a later edit, a newer deployment or a manifest that no longer covers the restored version is a conflict that keeps the files, and an unfinished or interrupted deployment returns to its previous state. No hook is installed, no runtime is called, nothing rolls out automatically and the runtime capability stays unsupported. `evaluation.adapter_deployment.check` exposes the refusals of `stage` without a journal.
