Document per-area scoring and route the question to it (part of #785, closes #800). New how-to material in `docs/score-by-capability-area.md`: a synthetic two-area project that `python3 scripts/build_area_score_example.py --out DIR` writes, the exact output of each `ahl score` command on it (all four evidence states, a single-area report with its multiplicity note, and the `score_area_undeclared` exit 2), how to read "how good is it for UX/UI?" and "what is its coding score?" from it, and what a score cannot tell you (aesthetics are out of scope, and few independent roots give wide intervals, 1.57 half-width at three roots). New `docs/author-area-graders.md` covers the grader contract (JSON envelope, exit 0, 1 or 2), objective checks (DOM assertions, accessibility rules, a screenshot diff against a golden image rendered by the grader, never a model), the control states and the review declarations, with a tested example grader and five controls under `examples/area-grader/`. New `docs/area-reference.md` lists the registry limits and typed reasons, the four evidence states across `ahl score` and the area gate, and the receipt keys of `ahl-score-v1` and `ahl-area-gate-v1`; its limits, reasons, states and keys are derived from the code by tests. The single skill's per-area routing no longer tells an agent to look for `score` in `ahl --help`: it names the full `ahl score . --spec ... --records ... --area X` command, what to say when no recorded experiment exists or the install predates the command, the state to quote before a number, and `--area X` on the invoke line to aim an improvement. Activation metadata and the always-loaded size budget are unchanged. The invocation reference and the docs index link the new pages.
